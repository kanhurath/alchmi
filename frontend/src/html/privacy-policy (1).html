Alchmi ("Alchmi", "we", "us", "our") provides corporate performance assessments, the Alchmi C-Growth Analysis™, leadership retreats, and related consultation services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through alchmi.com and our consultation booking process, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1. Introduction

By using our website or booking a consultation with Alchmi, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this Policy, please do not use our website or services, and contact us with your concerns using the details in Section 13.

This Policy applies to visitors of alchmi.com, prospective and existing clients who book consultations or retreats, and individuals who otherwise contact Alchmi through our forms, email, or phone.

2. Information we collect

We collect information in three broad categories: information you give us directly, information generated when you book and pay for a consultation, and information collected automatically as you browse our website.

CategoryExamples
Identity & contact details Full name, work email address, phone number, company name, job title/designation
Booking & engagement details Consultation type selected, preferred dates/times, notes you provide about your organization's requirements, correspondence with our team
Payment-related information Billing name, billing address, transaction amount, order and payment reference IDs generated by our payment partner, Razorpay (see Section 4)
Technical & usage data IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, referring website, approximate location (city/country), cookie identifiers

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information (such as health data, financial account numbers, or government identity numbers) through our website. If you share such information with us in the course of a consultation, we handle it in accordance with this Policy and applicable law, and only for the purpose you provided it.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and confirm consultation bookings, including scheduling and sending reminders;
  • Process payments for consultation fees and issue receipts/invoices;
  • Communicate with you about your booking, engagement, or enquiry, including by email or phone;
  • Prepare for and deliver the C-Growth Analysis™, consultations, and leadership retreats you have booked;
  • Improve our website, services, and client experience;
  • Maintain the security of our website and prevent fraud or misuse of our payment systems;
  • Send you service-related updates and, where you have opted in, occasional insights or updates about Alchmi's services; and
  • Comply with applicable legal, tax, and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Payment processing via Razorpay

Consultation fees are processed securely through Razorpay, a licensed and PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. When you pay for a consultation:

  • Your card, UPI, netbanking, or wallet details are entered directly into Razorpay's secure checkout and are never stored or seen by Alchmi's servers;
  • Alchmi retains only the payment outcome (success, failure, or pending), the transaction/order reference ID, the amount paid, and the payment date, in order to confirm your booking and maintain financial records;
  • Razorpay processes your payment information under its own privacy policy and applicable payment industry standards. We encourage you to review Razorpay's privacy policy for details on how it handles payment data.

5. Cookies & tracking technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, understand how visitors use our website, and improve site performance. These may include:

  • Essential cookies — required for core website functionality, such as maintaining your session while booking a consultation;
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand aggregate visitor behavior so we can improve our content and layout;
  • Preference cookies — remember choices you've made, such as language or region.

Most browsers let you control or disable cookies through their settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect your ability to book a consultation or use certain website features.

6. Data sharing & disclosure

We share personal information only where necessary, and with the following categories of recipients:

  • Payment processor: Razorpay, to process consultation fee payments;
  • Service providers: hosting providers, email/communication tools, scheduling tools, and analytics providers who process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations;
  • Professional advisors: our accountants, auditors, or legal counsel, where reasonably necessary;
  • Legal & regulatory authorities: where required to comply with law, a court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Alchmi, our clients, or others;
  • Business transfers: if Alchmi is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, client information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to confidentiality commitments.

We do not share your personal information with third parties for their own independent marketing purposes.

7. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy, including:

  • For the duration of your engagement with Alchmi and a reasonable period afterward to handle follow-up queries;
  • For financial and transaction records, for the period required under applicable tax and accounting regulations;
  • For website analytics data, in aggregated or anonymized form where possible.

When personal information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to securely delete or anonymize it.

8. Data security

We apply reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction, including restricted access to client data, secure connections (HTTPS) on our website, and reliance on a PCI-DSS compliant payment partner for all payment processing.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. While we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights & choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal or contractual retention requirements;
  • Withdraw consent for optional communications, such as marketing updates, at any time;
  • Object to or request that we restrict certain processing of your information;
  • Lodge a complaint with a relevant data protection authority, where applicable.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 13. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe and in accordance with applicable law.

10. Children's privacy

Alchmi's website and services are intended for business professionals and organizations. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

11. International data transfers

Alchmi is based in India, and personal information is generally processed and stored within India or by service providers operating under comparable safeguards. Where we or our service providers process information outside your home country, we take reasonable steps to ensure it is handled in accordance with this Policy and applicable data protection law.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when this Policy was last revised. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Material changes will be communicated through a notice on our website or by email, where appropriate.

13. Contact us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests relating to this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, please reach out to us.
