We understand that plans change. This policy explains what happens if you need to cancel or reschedule a paid consultation, C-Growth Analysis™ engagement, or leadership retreat, and how refunds are calculated and processed.

1. Overview

This Refund & Cancellation Policy applies to all Services booked and paid for through alchmi.com, where payment is processed via Razorpay. It should be read together with our Terms & Conditions.

Refund eligibility depends on how much notice is given before the scheduled consultation, assessment session, or retreat date, as set out below.

2. Cancellation by you

If you need to cancel a confirmed Booking, the amount refunded depends on when you notify us relative to your scheduled date:

Notice given before scheduled dateRefund
72 hours or more Full refund of the consultation fee
24–72 hours 50% refund of the consultation fee
Less than 24 hours, or no-show No refund

For multi-day leadership retreats, cancellation windows may be longer given venue and logistics commitments; the specific window and refund percentage for a given retreat will be confirmed to you at the time of booking.

3. Rescheduling

If you'd prefer to reschedule rather than cancel, we're happy to help wherever possible:

  • Requests to reschedule made 24 hours or more before the scheduled date can generally be accommodated at no extra cost, subject to availability, and will not count as a cancellation.
  • Requests made with less than 24 hours' notice will be handled on a best-effort basis and may be treated as a cancellation under Section 2 if a suitable alternative slot cannot be arranged.
  • Each Booking may be rescheduled up to one time free of charge; further changes may be treated as a new Booking.

4. Cancellation by Alchmi

If Alchmi needs to cancel or postpone a confirmed Booking — for example, due to consultant unavailability, unforeseen circumstances, or venue issues for a retreat — you will be offered, at your choice:

  • A full refund of the Fees paid for the affected Booking; or
  • A rescheduled date at no additional cost.

We will make reasonable efforts to notify you as early as possible in such situations.

5. Refund process & timelines

Once a refund is approved:

  • Refunds are issued through Razorpay to the original payment method used at the time of booking (card, UPI, netbanking, or wallet).
  • Approved refunds are typically initiated within 3–5 business days of approval.
  • Depending on your bank or payment provider, it may take an additional 5–7 business days for the refunded amount to reflect in your account, in line with Razorpay's standard settlement timelines.
  • You will receive an email confirmation once your refund has been initiated, including the reference ID for your records.

6. Non-refundable circumstances

The following are generally not eligible for a refund:

  • Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice, or failure to attend a scheduled consultation without prior notice ("no-show");
  • Consultations, assessments, or retreat sessions that have already been delivered in full;
  • Dissatisfaction with advisory recommendations or outcomes, where the Service was delivered as described — Alchmi's Services are advisory in nature and do not guarantee specific business results (see our Terms & Conditions);
  • Any portion of a retreat fee explicitly identified as non-refundable at the time of booking (for example, third-party venue or travel costs already committed on your behalf).

7. How to request a cancellation or refund

To cancel, reschedule, or request a refund:

  • Email us at the address below with your name, booking/order reference ID, and the reason for your request; or
  • Use the cancellation link included in your original booking confirmation email, if available.

We will confirm receipt of your request and let you know the outcome, including the applicable refund amount if any, within a reasonable timeframe.
